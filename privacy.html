Privacy Policy — AusTax Receipts

Last updated: 8 November 2026

The developer of AusTax Receipts (ABN 59 538 583 378) ("we", "us") operates the AusTax Receipts mobile application ("the App"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

The short version

AusTax Receipts is designed to be private. Your receipts, photos, and trip data stay on your device. We do not run servers that collect, store, or sell your data.

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Photos of receipts you capture or import
  • Receipt details you enter (vendor, date, amounts, GST, category, notes)
  • Vehicle/trip log entries and onsite work shifts
  • Saved departure addresses (e.g. Home, Company, Warehouse)
  • App settings

This data is never transmitted to us. If you delete the App, this data is removed with it (unless you have exported a backup yourself).

Backups you create

If you use "Export Backup", the App creates a file and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes (iCloud Drive, Files, email, etc.) is your choice and is governed by that service's privacy policy, not ours.

Camera and photo access

The App requests camera and photo-library access solely so you can attach receipt images. Images are processed on your device and stored locally. We do not upload them.

Location and addresses

The App uses your device location only when you clock in at a job site or tap "Use my current location". It is used to fill in an address, which iOS looks up through Apple's location services. The App does not track your location in the background.

To suggest addresses as you type and to calculate the driving distance between your departure address and work address, the App uses Apple Maps (Apple's MapKit service built into iOS). The address text you type and the chosen locations are sent to Apple to answer those requests, under Apple's privacy policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not receive this data, and it is not shared with any other company. Calculated distances are remembered on your device so the same trip is not looked up twice. You can always type a distance yourself instead.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your payment details. Subscription status is managed via Apple and our payment provider RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and your subscription status to unlock features — it does not receive your receipts or personal financial data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics and tracking

The App does not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not collect personal information for marketing.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Because data lives on your device, you control it. You can delete all data at any time via Settings → Delete All Data, or by deleting the App.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
