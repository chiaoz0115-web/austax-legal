Privacy Policy — AusTax Receipts

Last updated: 17 June 2026

The developer of AusTax Receipts (ABN 59 538 583 378) ("we", "us") operates the AusTax Receipts

mobile application ("the App"). This policy explains how the App handles your information.

The short version

AusTax Receipts is designed to be private. **Your receipts, photos, and trip data

stay on your device.** We do not run servers that collect, store, or sell your data.

Information stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your device only:

  • Photos of receipts you capture or import
  • Receipt details you enter (vendor, date, amounts, GST, category, notes)
  • Vehicle/trip log entries
  • App settings

This data is never transmitted to us. If you delete the App, this data is removed

with it (unless you have exported a backup yourself).

Backups you create

If you use "Export Backup", the App creates a file and hands it to the iOS share

sheet. Where that file goes (iCloud Drive, Files, email, etc.) is your choice and is

governed by that service's privacy policy, not ours.

Camera and photo access

The App requests camera and photo-library access solely so you can attach receipt

images. Images are processed on your device and stored locally. We do not upload them.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. We never see your

payment details. Subscription status is managed via Apple and our payment provider

RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous identifier and your subscription status to

unlock features — it does not receive your receipts or personal financial data.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics and tracking

The App does not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not

collect personal information for marketing.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

Because data lives on your device, you control it. You can delete all data at any time

via Settings → Delete All Data, or by deleting the App.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated"

date above.
